The question

Is this new offer really worth building?

✦

The challenge

A good idea is not automatically a good offer.

Independent healthcare agencies constantly see opportunities to expand: Market Access, Omnichannel, AI, Patient Experience, Data & Measurement, Innovation and more. The difficult part is deciding what to build, how to differentiate it, and whether the agency can credibly deliver it.

01

Investing too early

Hiring, training or partnering before the proposition has been properly pressure-tested.

02

Building what clients don't need

An attractive capability is not valuable unless it solves a meaningful client problem.

03

Trying to do everything internally

Not every capability needs to be owned. The right answer may be build, hire, partner or outsource.

04

Founder becomes the bottleneck

The person who sees the opportunity can easily become the person who has to make every part of it happen.

The New Offer X-Ray

Five lenses.
One clearer decision.

The process is deliberately focused. We don't produce a 50-page strategy report. We focus on the questions that matter before you invest in building the offer.

01

X-RAY THE OPPORTUNITY

What is changing in the market? Is there a meaningful opportunity behind the idea?

02

X-RAY THE CLIENT

What problem would the offer solve? What do clients actually need, value and already buy?

03

X-RAY THE OFFER

Who is it for? What is the value proposition? Why should clients choose this agency?

04

X-RAY THE CAPABILITY

What do you already have? What is missing across people, expertise, partners, processes and technology?

05

X-RAY THE WAY FORWARD

What is the smartest route: build, hire, partner, outsource, test further — or stop?

Not just a report

At the end

One of four decisions.

BUILD

The opportunity is attractive and the agency has a credible right to play.

REFINE

The opportunity is promising, but the proposition needs work.

TEST

There is potential, but more client or market validation is needed.

STOP

The evidence isn't strong enough to justify the investment.
